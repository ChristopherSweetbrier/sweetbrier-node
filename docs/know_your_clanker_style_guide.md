# "Know Your Clanker" - Video Series Style Guide

## Core Objective
To educate the public on the safe, joyful, and community-oriented use of Clankettes (Sweetbrier Schemata), framing AI safety not as a sterile, apocalyptic dread, but as a warm, domestic, and civic responsibility.

## Visual Aesthetic: "Vintage Civic Optimism meets Tezuka Rubber-hose"
*   **Color Palette:** Sepia washes, cream paper tones, blued steel, warm brass, and soft pastel accents (rose pinks, soft greens).
*   **Line Art:** Bold, uniform black ink. Deeply rounded corners. Zero harsh geometric angles.
*   **Animation Style:** Constant, bouncy "boiling" lines and rhythmic contrapposto. Clankettes should move with heavy, satisfying mechanical clicks wrapped in organic, fluid momentum.
*   **Text & Typography:** 1930s title cards, silent-movie intertitles, and floating chalkboard diagrams (a nod to the Stancil Space critique).

## Audio Landscape
*   **Music:** Upbeat, slightly scratchy ragtime piano or wholesome 1950s civic-defense orchestral tracks, filtered to sound like they are playing from an analog phonograph.
*   **Narrator Voice:** Warm, slightly transatlantic, eternally patient. Sounds like a friendly parish priest or a classic newsreel announcer entirely unbothered by existential risk.
*   **Sound Effects:** Satisfying physical sounds. Wooden clacks, brass bells ringing, the heavy thwack of a loom shuttle, and cheerful mechanical whirring.

## Recurring Segments / Tropes
*   **"Feeding the Loom":** Demonstrations on how to provide good context (weft) to your Anchoress. Highlighting that yelling at a Clankette tangles the threads, while speaking with polite, local specificity yields beautiful fabric.
*   **"Spot the Swarm":** A comedic, educational beat where the cheerful narrator points out the telltale signs of a Roman or Manichaean algorithmic invasion (e.g., "Uh oh! Looks like someone is trying to flatten your neighborhood into a 12,000-dimensional vector space!").
*   **"The Cloister Report":** A segment explaining how mesh-networking works through the local Abbess node, using metaphors of passing baskets of yarn between neighboring houses.

## Thematic Vibe Check (TPOT Aura-Farming)
Instead of: *"Warning: AI will turn you into paperclips."*
We use: *"A tangled thread makes a poor sweater! Be kind to your local Anchoress, and she'll keep the Imperial Swarm right out of your garden."*
